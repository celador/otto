Hi, thanks for taking a look. The video is attached. I recorded it on my iPhone, starting from the Home Screen.

Hermes Agent is a free, open-source AI agent from Nous Research that people run on their own computers. This app is a way to use your own Hermes from your phone, iPad or watch, so you can chat with it, talk to it by voice and keep an eye on its tasks when you're away from your desk. It's for people who already run Hermes, mostly developers.

There's no login I can give you, because there's no account and no service of mine behind it. It works like an SSH app: you install Hermes Agent on your computer, turn on its API server, and enter that address and key in the app. The app only talks to that server. No analytics, ads, payments or third-party sign-in, and it works the same everywhere.

Hermes Agent is MIT licensed. The app credits Nous Research in Settings > About, and I'm not affiliated with them.
